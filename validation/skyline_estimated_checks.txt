6. Against RevBayes 1.3.2 on a fixed tree (est_rb_check.py; rates lambda_i, mu_i, psi_i ~ Exp(10)).
   a) Fixed shift times (bears, shifts 5,15): posterior means of all 9 rates agree (largest |z| 1.90); stepping-stone ln marginal
      likelihood ffbd -201.050 / -201.035, RevBayes -201.048 / -201.092.
   b) Estimated shift times: RevBayes 1.3.2 cannot be used. With a stochastic timeline (t ~ dnUniform, timeline := v(t, ...) or sort(t)),
      dnBDSTP is recomputed after setValue (lnProbability -185.740 at t = 6.5 and -191.699 at t = 4, equal to ffbd's lnFBD to 1e-9),
      but not during MCMC: the logged Likelihood stays at its start value (-185.740 in all 9001 samples of a 100,000-generation run with
      mvSlide on t), so the shift time samples its prior (KS against the exact posterior p 8e-112). The estimated-time cases are therefore
      checked against exact numerical integration instead (7).
7. Exact numerical integration (bears tree fixed, rates fixed at lambda 0.12,0.10,0.16, mu 0.33,0.24,0.03, psi 0.05,0.005,0.002; origin 50):
   Input: validation/est_grid_bears_input.txt; script: scripts/est_grid_check.py (one | two).
   a) One time, window 3:10 (second shift fixed at 20); grid of 1400 cells (0.005 Myr) of ffbd's lnFBD.
      Posterior: ffbd 2 chains x 10,000 samples, KS against the grid CDF p 0.74 (means 6.393; exact 6.390).
      Marginal likelihood (--ss 50 --ss-power fbd): -186.5766, -186.5717; exact -186.5756.
   b) free:2 on [0, 33.9] (both times free, ordered); grid of 0.01 Myr (5,744,355 cells; the diagonal, 0.03% of the area, left out),
      evaluated with the test option --eval-shifts. ln Z grid -188.9435; ffbd stepping-stone -188.9499, -188.9423.
      Posterior (2 chains, 4000 samples thinned x5): T1 exact mean 6.302, ffbd 6.296, KS p 0.37; T2 exact 18.667, ffbd 18.687, KS p 0.058.
      The density jumps where a shift time crosses a node or fossil age, so the grid must be fine: on a 0.1 Myr grid the same chains
      seemed off (T2 KS p < 0.001).
