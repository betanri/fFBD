Shift-time moves (shift_data, shift_relocate) and heated chains (--heated): checks
================================================================================
Scripts: scripts/sbc_sky.py (SBC; `analyze DIR` prints the tests), scripts/est_grid_check.py (exact integration),
tests/run_tests.sh (cache checks, threads). Chi-square on 20 bins and KS on the normalised ranks; Bonferroni threshold
0.05 / number of quantities (17 to 21 quantities).

1. No new option: same output as the previous release
   24 run settings (4 examples: default, acln, ln with burn-in, control, fixed shifts 3,9, igr with no data):
   .log and .trees byte-identical; stepping-stone .ml.tsv identical. Runs with estimated shifts change (new moves).

2. Cache check after every proposal (--check-every-step)
   New moves: 105 runs (3 examples x 5 clocks x free:1, free:2, free:3, windows, fixed + window, free:2 lambda,mu,
   free:3 psi only), no error.
   Heated chains: 72 runs (4 examples x 3 clocks x no shift / free:2 / fixed shifts x heat on fbd / all), every copy
   checked, no error. Output with 1 and 4 threads byte-identical; checkpoint + resume byte-identical.

3. Prior (FBD density off, 20 million proposals, 4,000 samples)
   All moves: shift times KS p 0.40 to 0.95; rates p 0.01 to 0.99 (41 tests).
   Each new move alone (tree and other rates fixed): shift times KS p 0.17 to 0.95; psi p 0.45 to 0.61.

4. Exact numerical integration (bears tree and rates fixed)
   One time in a 3-10 Ma window: KS p 0.43 (6 seeds); marginal likelihood within 0.004 of exact.
   Two free times: marginal likelihood within 0.01 of exact; posterior against the 0.01 Myr grid, 20,000 samples,
   old and new moves: first time KS p 0.32 / 0.95, second 0.25 / 0.19.
   (The grid script now interpolates the grid CDF inside each 0.01 Myr cell. Before, it gave each sample the CDF at the
   top of its cell, which made the first time look slightly off, KS p 0.015 to 0.033, for the old and new moves alike.)
   Heated chains, one time: KS p 0.17; marginal likelihood -186.579 / -186.575 / -186.576 / -186.572, exact -186.576.
